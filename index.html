Grouper: random student groups for teachers

Grouper is a free classroom tool for teachers. Take the roll, choose how many students go in each group, and shuffle your class into random, colour-coded groups big enough to read on a projector.

Keep good pairs of students together and keep others apart, lock the groups you like and reshuffle the rest, then share the groups as a picture for a slide, as text, or as a printout.

Class lists stay in your browser: no accounts, no sign-up, and no student data sent to a server. Grouper works offline and can be added to the Home Screen.
